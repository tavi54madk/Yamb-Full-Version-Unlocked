## Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

Yamb, also known as Yet Another Mp4Box, is a versatile video editor and converter designed specifically for light formats such as MP4 and 3GP. It simplifies the process of preparing videos for mobile devices, including iPhones and iPads. Unlike many video editors that primarily handle raw files, Yamb is tailored to work directly with compressed formats. This makes it an ideal choice for users who need an efficient solution for video conversion and editing.
